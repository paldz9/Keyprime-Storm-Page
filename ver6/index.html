FAQ

Common Questions About Storm Damage

How do I know if my roof has storm damage?

If you suspect your roof has wind or hail damage, contact a roofing contractor like Keyprime because the only way to confirm such damage is to have a qualified inspector assess it. There are some indicators of potential damage that you can spot. Check the attic for water stains, black marks, or mold. Inspect your ceiling for stains. Look for granules in your gutters that would indicate your shingles were damaged. Older roofs are more susceptible to damage, so if your roof is 18 to 20 years old or older, you should consider roof replacement.

How do I get storm damage roof repair covered by insurance?

Once you suspect your roof has storm damage, contact your insurance provider to confirm what type of policy you have, how long it will take to process a claim, and if estimates will be required for repairs. You have two years after the date of damage to report it to your insurance company. Once you contact your insurer, contact Keyprime. Our qualified inspectors are trained to identify storm damage and our experienced staff will work with insurance to get storm damage to your roof repaired quickly and correctly.

What type of hail damage requires roof repair?

Depending on the size of the hail damage, different types of repairs will be more useful. The type of repair would depend on how deep and wide the hole is. A roofing contractor will be able to determine if a repair or replacement is needed.

Why should I have my roofing contractor work directly with my insurance provider?

Keyprime not only inspects roofs for storm damage, but can also work directly with your insurance provider to get the damage repaired quickly with no upfront cost to you. Keyprime employs a team of experienced professionals that specialize in working through insurance claims to make sure they are processed properly and on time.
