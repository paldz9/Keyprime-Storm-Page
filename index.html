FAQ

Common Questions About Storm Damage

Everything Minnesota homeowners need to know about storm damage repair, insurance claims, and getting your home restored.

How do I know if my roof has storm damage?

If you suspect your roof has wind or hail damage, contact a roofing contractor like Keyprime because the only way to confirm such damage is to have a qualified inspector assess it. Look for missing or cracked shingles, dented gutters, or granule loss in downspouts as early signs.

How do I get storm damage covered by insurance?

Once you suspect your roof has storm damage, contact your insurance provider to start a claim. In Minnesota, you have two years after the date of damage to report it. Keyprime can work directly with your insurance company to document the damage and streamline the claims process.

What type of hail damage requires roof repair?

Depending on the size of the hail, different types of repairs may be needed. Small hail may only cause cosmetic damage, while larger hailstones (1" or more) can crack shingles, damage underlayment, and compromise your roof's waterproofing. A roofing contractor will determine if a repair or full replacement is needed.

Why should my contractor work directly with my insurance?

Keyprime not only inspects roofs for storm damage but can also work directly with your insurance provider to get the damage repaired quickly with no upfront cost to you. This ensures proper documentation, prevents underpayment, and accelerates the entire process.

How soon should I act after a storm?

The sooner, the better. Unaddressed storm damage can lead to water intrusion, mold growth, and structural deterioration. Contact Keyprime as soon as it's safe to do so — we prioritize emergency storm response and can typically schedule an inspection within 24–48 hours.
